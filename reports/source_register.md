## Disclosure control for WLMDS demographics

The current NHS England page states that national totals 1-7 lead to subnational masking; where the national total is at least 8, subnational values 1-7 are masked; remaining values are rounded to the nearest 5. The project therefore stores a numeric value separately from `value_status` and never replaces `*` with zero or attempts reconstruction.

## Freshness caveat observed during build

The NHS publication calendar indicated a WLMDS release around the September 2026 operational publication, while the WLMDS landing page inspected on 15 September still exposed files to 26 July 2026. The pipeline trusts discoverable source files rather than inventing an unavailable August dataset and records the extraction timestamp for auditability.
